Privacy Policy for KametiMate

Effective date: November 3, 2026

KametiMate helps people organize savings committees and similar group arrangements. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you or your committee may provide:

  • account details, such as your email address and profile information;
  • committee details, member names and contact information, schedules, rules, invitations, and participation status;
  • contribution and payout records, payment confirmations, and notes;
  • payment details entered by an administrator, such as account names, bank account or routing details, payment usernames, links, and payment instructions;
  • discussion messages, voice notes, and information you send through support or bug-report forms, including attachments you choose to add.

Committee information and payment details may be visible to people who have access to that committee. Only enter information you are authorized to share.

How information is used

We use information to operate KametiMate, including to:

  • create and secure accounts and synchronize committee information;
  • show committee schedules, contributions, payouts, and reports;
  • send committee invitations and app notifications;
  • provide discussions, voice notes, support, and troubleshooting; and
  • maintain and improve the app.

Service providers and sharing

KametiMate uses Supabase for account and cloud app services and Firebase services for push notifications. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information. KametiMate does not move money or process payments; payments are made outside the app using the details users choose to share.

Device permissions and notifications

The app may request notification permission to deliver app notifications. It may also request microphone or file/photo access when you choose features that use voice notes or attachments. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service and maintain committee records. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email kametimate@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify your request. Some information may be retained when required by law or needed to resolve disputes or protect the service.

Security

We use safeguards intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

KametiMate is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as KametiMate changes. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email kametimate@gmail.com.
